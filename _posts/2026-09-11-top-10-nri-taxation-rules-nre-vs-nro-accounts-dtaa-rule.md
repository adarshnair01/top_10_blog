Non-Resident Indians earning foreign income must navigate Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAA) and Form 67 filings to avoid paying tax twice on the same earnings.

Managing NRI tax liability requires verifying physical stay days in India under Section 6, NRE tax exemptions, and foreign tax credit claims under Rule 128.

## 10. Determining NRI Residential Status under Section 6 of Income Tax Act

Individual classified as NRI if physical stay in India is under 182 days in financial year. Deemed Resident rule applies to Indian citizens paying zero tax in any foreign country. Key operational metrics show threshold: < 182 days in india | special rule: 120 days for income > ₹15 lakhs.

Threshold drops to 120 days if Indian-sourced taxable income exceeds ₹15 Lakhs. For best results, track arrival and departure passport stamps carefully every financial year.

## 9. NRE vs NRO Bank Account Tax Treatment Matrix

Interest earned on NRE (Non-Resident External) savings & FDs is COMPLETELY TAX-FREE in India. TDS of 30% deducted automatically by bank on NRO interest payouts. Key operational metrics show nre interest: 100% tax-free | nro interest: taxable at 30% + surcharge | repatriation: nre (unlimited), nro ($1m).

Interest on NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) accounts is taxable at 30% + surcharge. For best results, submit DTAA benefit request to bank to reduce NRO TDS rate to 15%.

## 8. Claiming DTAA Benefits to Lower Indian TDS Rates (Form 10F & TRC)

NRIs can claim lower TDS rates under DTAA for Indian rental and interest income. File Form 10F electronically on Indian Income Tax portal. Key operational metrics show requirement: tax residency certificate (trc) + form 10f | lower rate: 10-15%.

Obtain Tax Residency Certificate (TRC) from foreign country's tax department (e.g. IRS Form 6166). For best results, submit TRC and Form 10F copy to Indian bank/tenant to reduce TDS.

## 7. Rule 128 Foreign Tax Credit (FTC) & Form 67 Filing Protocol

NRIs paying tax in foreign country can set off foreign tax paid against Indian tax liability. Failure to file Form 67 before ITR disallows foreign tax credit completely. Key operational metrics show mandate: file form 67 before itr | benefit: tax credit for foreign paid taxes.

Must submit Form 67 online on Income Tax portal BEFORE filing annual ITR. For best results, attach official foreign tax return or foreign tax payment receipt copy.

## 6. Taxation of Indian Rental Income for NRIs & Tenant TDS Rules

Tenants renting property from NRIs MUST deduct 31.2% TDS under Section 195 regardless of rent amount. NRI files Indian ITR to claim refund of excess TDS deducted. Key operational metrics show tenant obligation: deduct 31.2% tds under sec 195 | certificate: form 16a | exemption: lower deduction cert.

Tenant deposits TDS using TAN number and issues Form 16A to NRI landlord. Apply for Lower Deduction Certificate under Section 197 to reduce upfront TDS.

## 5. Taxation of Capital Gains on Indian Property Sale by NRIs

Buyer of NRI property must deduct 20% LTCG (or 30% STCG) + surcharge TDS on GROSS sale price. NRI seller applies for Section 197 Certificate to compute TDS on actual capital gain only. Key operational metrics show tds rate: 20% ltcg / 30% stcg + surcharge | purchaser duty: deduct tds on gross sale value.

Differs from resident sellers where TDS is 1% under Section 194-IA. For best results, reinvest capital gains in Section 54 residential house to claim tax exemption.

## 4. Inheritance Tax & Gift Tax Rules for NRIs Receiving Property in India

India does not levy any Inheritance Tax on inherited cash, property, or stocks. Subsequent sale of inherited property attracts capital gains tax. Key operational metrics show inheritance tax: zero in india | gift tax: tax-free from specified relatives.

Gifts of Indian property or cash from linear relatives (parents, siblings) are 100% tax-free. For best results, cost of acquisition for capital gains is taken as cost to original owner.

## 3. Taxation of Global Income for Returning NRIs (RNOR Status)

Returning NRIs qualify for RNOR status for 2-3 financial years after moving back. Only income earned or received within India is taxable during RNOR window. Key operational metrics show status: resident but not ordinarily resident | window: 2 to 3 years | tax: foreign income tax-free.

Global foreign income remains TAX-FREE in India during RNOR period. For best results, ideal window to liquidate foreign stocks and offshore assets tax-free.

## 2. FEMA Compliance & Repatriation of NRO Funds up to $1 Million

NRIs can repatriate up to USD 1 Million per financial year from NRO account balances. Submit Form 15CA online on IT portal along with Form 15CB. Key operational metrics show limit: usd 1,000,000 / financial year | documents: form 15ca & form 15cb | ca cert required.

Requires CA certificate (Form 15CB) certifying all Indian taxes paid. For best results, aD Bank processes wire transfer upon verifying Form 15CA/CB.

## 1. Filing Income Tax Returns (ITR) in India as an NRI

NRI must file Indian ITR if taxable income in India exceeds basic exemption limit. NRIs do NOT need to declare foreign assets in Schedule FA unless resident. Key operational metrics show mandatory trigger: taxable income > ₹2.5 lakhs in india | form: itr-2 | schedule al disclosure.

NRIs MUST file using ITR-2 (or ITR-3 if business income); ITR-1 Sahaj cannot be used. For best results, claim TDS refunds deducted on NRO interest and property transactions.
