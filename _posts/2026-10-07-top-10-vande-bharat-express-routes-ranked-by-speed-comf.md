India's semi-high-speed train revolution, led by the indigenous Vande Bharat Express (Train 18), has fundamentally altered intercity rail travel across the subcontinent. Boasting operational acceleration of 0 to 100 km/h in just 52 seconds, sealed gangways, automated plug doors, and bio-vacuum toilets, these trainsets demand a fresh logistics playbook for travelers.

Bypassing congested national highways and traditional superfast train delays, navigating Vande Bharat services requires precise knowledge of platform allocations, catering meal windows, and baggage rack dimensions.

## 10. New Delhi to Varanasi Vande Bharat Express (Train No. 22436)

Cruises at 130 km/h across UP plains. Departure at 06:00 AM from NDLS Platform 16. Cuts travel time to Varanasi by 4 hours over Superfast trains. Key operational metrics show 759 km in 8 hrs | Avg Speed: 94.88 km/h.

Operates at peak efficiency with 99.1% punctuality. For best results, exit via Platform 1 (Cantonment side) at Varanasi Junction for prepaid autos.

## 9. New Delhi to Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra (Train No. 22439)

Designed for religious transit to Katra. Halts at Ambala, Ludhiana, Jammu Tawi. Eliminates overnight train stays, reaching Katra by 2:00 PM. Key operational metrics show 655 km in 8 hrs | Avg Speed: 81.88 km/h.

Non-veg free menu aligned with pilgrimage traditions. Keep Yatra RFID card handy for Katra station exit gates.

## 8. New Delhi to Amb Andaura (Train No. 22447)

Connects NCR with Himachal foothills via Ambala, Chandigarh, Anandpur Sahib. Direct high-speed gateway to Garhwal and Kangra Valley. Key operational metrics show 415 km in 5 hrs 15 mins | Avg Speed: 79.04 km/h.

Reaches Chandigarh in under 3 hours from NDLS. For best results, pre-book cabs at Una Himachal station to bypass local bus queues.

## 7. Kasaragod to Thiruvananthapuram (Train No. 20633)

Traverses Kerala north-to-south via Kannur, Kozhikode, Thrissur, Ernakulam. Cuts intra-Kerala travel time from 12 hours down to 8 hours. Key operational metrics show 587 km in 8 hrs 05 mins | Avg Speed: 72.61 km/h.

Consistently reports over 180% booking occupancy. For best results, be ready for quick 2-minute stops at Ernakulam Town.

## 6. KSR Bengaluru to Dharwad (Train No. 20661)

Links Karnataka tech capital with Hubballi-Dharwad industrial hub. Connects Dharwad university hubs to Bangalore startups. Key operational metrics show 489 km in 6 hrs 25 mins | Avg Speed: 76.26 km/h.

Replaces long 9-hour bus journeys on NH 48. For best results, board via Metro Skywalk directly to Platform 8 at KSR Bengaluru.

## 5. Mumbai Central to Ahmedabad (Train No. 20901)

Operates across Western Railway trunk line via Surat and Vadodara. Reliable alternative to Mumbai-Surat highway traffic. Key operational metrics show 493 km in 5 hrs 25 mins | Avg Speed: 91.01 km/h.

Holds one of the highest speed ratings at 91 km/h. For best results, exit via Surat East Gate to avoid diamond market traffic.

## 4. Secunderabad to Visakhapatnam (Train No. 20833)

Connects Telangana capital with Vizag port city via Vijayawada. Essential business transit link for IT and maritime sectors. Key operational metrics show 698 km in 8 hrs 30 mins | Avg Speed: 82.11 km/h.

Reaches 130 km/h top speed on Vijayawada section. Book app cabs while passing Simhachalam for zero wait time.

## 3. Anand Vihar Terminal to Dehradun (Train No. 20847)

Connects NCR to Shivalik foothills via Haridwar. Fastest rail gateway for Himalayan trekkers. Key operational metrics show 302 km in 4 hrs 45 mins | Avg Speed: 63.58 km/h.

Bypasses Delhi-Dehradun expressway construction delays. For best results, choose Executive Class for oversized rucksack storage.

## 2. Howrah to Puri (Train No. 22895)

Links Bengal metro core to Odisha beach pilgrimage. Enables same-day weekend seaside getaways. Key operational metrics show 500 km in 6 hrs 25 mins | Avg Speed: 77.92 km/h.

Reduces Howrah-Puri transit to under 6.5 hours. Book left-side window seats for Mahanadi River sunrise views.

## 1. Chennai Central to Coimbatore (Train No. 20643)

Connects Chennai to Salem and Coimbatore industrial belt. Cuts travel time by 75 minutes over Shatabdi. Key operational metrics show 495 km in 5 hrs 50 mins | Avg Speed: 84.86 km/h.

Fastest rail connection across Tamil Nadu inland trunk line. For best results, choose C1 or C2 coaches for quick exit at Coimbatore.
