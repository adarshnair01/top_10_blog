Indian Railways specifies luggage weight limits per coach class and regulates carrying pets, bicycles, and commercial goods inside passenger compartments.

Navigating railway luggage rules requires understanding luggage office booking procedures, pet dog coupe allocation rules, and excess baggage fee slabs.

---

## 10. Free Luggage Allowance by Train Class & Marginal Allowance

*Key Specs: 1st AC: 70 kg | 2nd AC / Executive: 50 kg | 3rd AC / CC: 40 kg | Sleeper: 40 kg*

First AC passengers get maximum 70 kg free baggage allowance per ticket. Marginal allowance grants extra 10-15 kg buffer before imposing excess luggage penalty.

> **Operational Insight:** 2nd AC and Executive Class allow 50 kg; 3rd AC, Chair Car, and Sleeper allow 40 kg. Dimensions must not exceed 100cm x 60cm x 25cm.

## 9. Booking Excess Baggage & Luggage Van (Parcel Office) Rules

*Key Specs: Rate: 1.5x Parcel Rate for Excess | Action: Book at Luggage Office | Penalty: 6x Rate if Caught*

Luggage exceeding free allowance must be booked at station Luggage/Parcel Office. Unbooked excess luggage detected by ticket collector on train penalized at 6x rate.

> **Operational Insight:** Excess baggage booked in advance charged at standard parcel rate. Book heavy trunks at parcel office 2 hours before train departure.

## 8. Rules for Travelling with Pet Dogs & Cats on Indian Railways

*Key Specs: Class: 1st AC / Executive (Full Coupe/Cabin) | Booking: Parcel Counter | Alternate: Luggage Van*

Pet dogs allowed inside passenger coach ONLY in 1st AC / Executive Class if booking entire coupe (2-berth) or cabin (4-berth). If traveling in 2AC/3AC, pets must travel in dog box inside Brake Van/Luggage Van.

> **Operational Insight:** Requires veterinary fit-to-travel certificate and rabies vaccination card. Submit written request to Chief Reservation Supervisor for 1st AC coupe allotment.

## 7. Carrying Bicycles, Folding Cycles & Two-Wheelers on Trains

*Key Specs: Folding Cycle: Cabin Luggage | Motorbike: Parcel Van Packing | Requirement: Empty Petrol Tank*

Folding bicycles fitting luggage rack dimensions permitted inside coach as personal luggage. Motorcycle petrol tank must be COMPLETELY DRAINED before parcel booking.

> **Operational Insight:** Standard bicycles and motorcycles must be booked as parcel cargo in Luggage Van. Carrying motorbike with petrol attracts heavy criminal penalty under Railway Act.

## 6. Prohibited & Hazardous Items on Indian Railways

*Key Specs: Prohibited: Explosives, Crackers, Gas Cylinders, Acid, Kerosene | Penalty: 3 Yrs Jail / ₹1,000*

Carrying inflammable goods (firecrackers, gas cylinders, petrol, acid) strictly prohibited. Imprisonment up to 3 years or fine up to ₹1,000 or both.

> **Operational Insight:** Violators prosecuted under Section 164 of Indian Railways Act 1989. RPF conducts surprise luggage scanning at major railway station entry gates.

## 5. Carrying Commercial Goods & Merchandise in Passenger Coaches

*Key Specs: Rule: Personal Effects Only | Penalty: Commercial Parcel Rate + Penalty | Action: Seizure*

Passenger coaches meant exclusively for personal travel luggage. TTE penalizes commercial traders carrying trade goods in passenger compartments.

> **Operational Insight:** Carrying commercial merchandise bundles inside AC/Sleeper coaches is illegal. Book commercial merchandise via Indian Railways Freight/Parcel booking portal.

## 4. Luggage Storage Dimensions & Under-Berth Space Rules

*Key Specs: Dimensions: Max 100 cm x 60 cm x 25 cm | Location: Under Lower Berth | Right: Berth Passenger Priority*

Baggage stored under lower berth must not obstruct aisle walking space. Oversized trunks exceeding 100cm x 60cm x 25cm must be booked in brake van.

> **Operational Insight:** Passenger holding lower berth has priority right to under-berth storage space. Keep high-value laptops and handbags near your headrest area.

## 3. Cloak Room & Station Luggage Locker Rules

*Key Specs: Requirement: Confirmed Train Ticket + Lockable Bag | Fee: ₹30-50 per 24 Hrs | Max Stay: 7 Days*

Major railway stations offer Cloak Room facility for short-term luggage storage. Luggage must be securely locked with physical lock; open bags rejected.

> **Operational Insight:** Must produce confirmed train ticket and government photo ID. Maximum storage duration is 7 days.

## 2. Carrying Food, Gas Stoves & Cooking Equipment

*Key Specs: Permitted: Packed Food, Electric Kettle | Prohibited: Gas Stoves, Kerosene Burners*

Packed cooked food, dry snacks, and electric kettles permitted. Boiling food using open flame or gas stove inside train coach is a criminal offense.

> **Operational Insight:** Carrying portable gas stoves, LPG cylinders, or kerosene burners is illegal. Utilize IRCTC e-catering service to order fresh food delivered to your seat.

## 1. Claiming Compensation for Lost or Stolen Luggage on Trains

*Key Specs: Action: File GRP FIR Form | Claim: Railway Claims Tribunal (RCT) | Max Claim: ₹100/kg*

If luggage is stolen, request TTE for pre-printed GRP FIR complaint form. Compensation capped at statutory rate unless luggage value was declared during parcel booking.

> **Operational Insight:** File formal claim for loss of baggage with Railway Claims Tribunal (RCT). Always keep valuable jewelry, cash, and electronics in personal shoulder bag.
