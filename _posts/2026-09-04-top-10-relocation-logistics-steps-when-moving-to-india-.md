Relocating to India as a returning NRI or expat involves container shipping logistics, customs duty clearance, foreign bank account conversions, and tax residence planning.

Managing international relocation requires executing Transfer of Residence (TR) rules, shipping personal effects duty-free, and updating NRI banking status.

## 10. Executing Transfer of Residence (TR) Rules for Duty-Free Baggage Clearance

Returning NRIs living abroad for 2+ years can ship household goods duty-free. Customs allows container arrival within 1 month before or 2 months after NRI arrival. Key operational metrics show rule: customs baggage rules rule 6 | requirement: 2 years overseas stay | benefit: duty-free personal effects.

Covers used furniture, books, clothing, and personal appliances. For best results, submit copy of passport with foreign entry/exit stamps to customs broker.

## 9. Shipping Household Goods & Container Cargo Logistics (FCL vs LCL)

Choose Full Container Load (FCL) for full home relocation; LCL for smaller loads. Ensure detailed inventory list with serial numbers is prepared for customs. Key operational metrics show container: 20ft / 40ft fcl | lcl: shared shipping | timeline: 45-60 days transit.

Book international movers offering door-to-door customs clearance. For best results, take comprehensive marine cargo transit insurance covering door-to-door damage.

## 8. Converting Overseas Driving License to Indian Driving License

Some state RTOs issue Indian DL based on valid foreign license without driving test. Must pass basic online RTO traffic sign computer test. Key operational metrics show authority: regional rto | process: exemption from driving test (select countries) | requirement: dl verification.

Requires notarized copy of foreign DL, embassy verification, and medical Form 1A. Apply on Parivahan portal under 'Direct DL for Foreign License Holder'.

## 7. Redesignating Resident Bank Accounts to NRE/NRO Accounts

Notify Indian banks within 3 months of returning permanently to India. NRE accounts can be retained or converted to RFC (Resident Foreign Currency) accounts. Key operational metrics show mandate: fema regulations | timeline: within 3 months of return | action: bank status update.

Re-designate existing resident savings accounts to NRO accounts. For best results, rFC accounts allow holding foreign currency (USD/GBP/EUR) tax-free in India.

## 6. Customs Duty Rules on Bringing Used Electronics & Appliances

One used laptop per passenger imported duty-free regardless of age. Used home appliances (fridge, washing machine) imported under TR concessional duty slabs. Key operational metrics show concession: concessional duty tiers | exemption: 1 laptop duty-free | appliance cap: ₹5 lakhs.

Used television sets attract flat 38.5% customs duty without basic exemption. For best results, declare all electronics in Customs Baggage Declaration form upon arrival.

## 5. Planning Foreign Asset Disclosures & RNOR Tax Status

Returning NRIs enjoy RNOR tax status for 2-3 years after returning. Liquidate foreign stocks and offshore assets tax-free while in RNOR status. Key operational metrics show status: resident but not ordinarily resident | duration: 2 to 3 financial years | benefit: foreign income tax-free.

Global foreign income remains tax-free in India during RNOR period. For best results, consult Indian cross-border CA before transferring overseas investment portfolios.

## 4. Shipping Pets (Dogs/Cats) to India under Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)

Importing pets requires DGFT import license and AQCS NOC 7 days prior to travel. Mandatory 14-day home quarantine required upon arrival in India. Key operational metrics show permit: dgft import license | requirement: no objection certificate (aqcs) | cap: 2 pets per passenger.

Pets must have valid rabies vaccination and microchip ID. For best results, engage certified pet relocation agency to handle airline and AQCS paperwork.

## 3. Setting Up Local Mobile Connectivity & e-SIM Activation

Foreign passport holders and OCI cardholders can get prepaid/postpaid Indian SIM. e-SIM activation takes 2 hours after QR code scanning. Key operational metrics show provider: jio / airtel 5g | requirement: passport + oci/visa + local reference | activation: 2 hours.

Requires physical passport verification and local reference contact details at store. For best results, select postpaid plan for uninterrupted banking OTP delivery.

## 2. Opening Resident Demat & Trading Accounts for Stock Portfolio Transfers

Convert NRI PIS/Non-PIS Demat accounts to resident Demat accounts. Update KYC details with depository participant (broker). Key operational metrics show process: nri to resident demat conversion | authority: sebi / nsdl / cdsl | action: broker intimation.

Transfer Indian equity shares from NRE/NRO Demat to resident Demat account. For best results, prevents trading account freeze due to residency status mismatch.

## 1. School Admission Logistics for Children (IB / IGCSE vs CBSE)

Admit children returning from abroad into IB or Cambridge IGCSE accredited schools. Foreign school documents must be apostilled or endorsed by Indian Embassy. Key operational metrics show curricula: international baccalaureate / cambridge igcse | process: document attestation | window: jan - april.

Requires attested school transcripts and transfer certificate from foreign school. Apply for school admissions 6 months prior to academic year start.
