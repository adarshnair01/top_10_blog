Purchasing real estate in India involves complex property title verification, stamp duty calculations, and municipal property tax mutation. Understanding legal paperwork protects buyers against property fraud.

Evaluating property paperwork requires referencing Registration Act 1908, Transfer of Property Act 1882, municipal corporation bylaws, and RERA regulations.

## 10. What is Property Mutation (Khata Transfer) and why is it distinct from Registration?

Registration transfers ownership; Mutation updates municipal tax register. Essential for obtaining electricity connections and selling property later. Key operational metrics show purpose: municipal tax records | authority: revenue dept / municipal corp | protection: prevents title fraud.

Without mutation, property tax bills continue issuing in seller's name. Apply for mutation within 60 days of sale deed registration.

## 9. How to verify Encumbrance Certificate (EC) before buying property?

EC reflects all registered sales, mortgages, and legal claims on property. Ensures property is free from bank liens or legal disputes. Key operational metrics show period: 13 to 30 years | authority: sub-registrar office (sro) | sign: nil encumbrance.

Obtain Form 15 (Encumbrances exist) or Form 16 (Nil Encumbrance). For best results, verify EC records online via state registration portal.

## 8. How to convert Agricultural Land to Non-Agricultural (NA) use?

Agricultural land cannot be used for residential/commercial construction without NA order. Pay NA conversion premium and development charges. Key operational metrics show process: land use conversion | authority: district collector / revenue dept | fee: na premium.

Apply to District Collector with land survey map and title chain. For best results, verify NA order layout approval by town planning department.

## 7. What is a Occupancy Certificate (OC) and can you legally live without it?

OC certifies building constructed according to approved Sanction Plan. Banks refuse home loans for flats lacking valid OC. Key operational metrics show status: mandatory for living | authority: municipal corp / development authority | risk: disconnection.

Living in building without OC is legally unauthorized; risks utility cut-off. For best results, insist on builder producing OC before taking flat possession.

## 6. How to check if real estate project is RERA Registered?

Search project RERA registration number on state RERA website. RERA forces builders to park 70% funds in dedicated escrow account. Key operational metrics show portal: state rera web portal | mandatory: projects over 500 sq m or 8 flats | verification: rera reg no.

Check sanctioned floor plans, promoter background, and completion timeline. For best results, never pay advance money for non-RERA registered projects.

## 5. How to calculate Stamp Duty and Registration Fees accurately?

Stamp duty calculated on higher of circle rate (guideline value) or agreement value. Pay stamp duty via e-Stamping portal or franking banks. Key operational metrics show value: circle rate vs sale value (whichever is higher) | rate: 4%-8% stamp + 1% reg | discount: female buyers.

Many states offer 1-2% stamp duty discount for female buyers. For best results, calculate circle rate using official municipal portal calculator.

## 4. How to resolve Khata A vs Khata B property classification in Bangalore?

A Khata properties are fully legal with approved plans and OC. B Khata flats face bank loan difficulties and resale challenges. Key operational metrics show status: municipal classification | authority: bbmp | limitation: bank loan restrictions.

B Khata properties have building violations or unreleased layouts. For best results, convert B Khata to A Khata by paying betterment charges if eligible.

## 3. How to execute a Gift Deed for property transfer between family members?

Transferring property via Gift Deed requires registration at Sub-Registrar office. Gift Deed is irrevocable once accepted by donee. Key operational metrics show stamp duty: concessional rate | registration: mandatory at sro | tax: tax-free under sec 56.

Concessional stamp duty applies for transfers to immediate relatives in many states. Ensure donee signs Gift Deed accepting transfer during registration.

## 2. How to remove Bank Lien (Home Loan NOC) from property records after loan closure?

Obtain original property documents, NOC, and No Due Certificate from bank. Ensure bank updates CERSAI registry database clearing mortgage record. Key operational metrics show document: deed of reconveyance / noc | authority: sro & cersai | action: lien removal.

Register Deed of Reconveyance at Sub-Registrar office where loan was marked. For best results, collect original sale deed title chain from bank safe vault.

## 1. How to handle power of attorney (POA) property sales safely?

Supreme Court ruled POA sales do not confer legal ownership title. Verify POA grantor is alive and POA has not been revoked. Key operational metrics show risk: high fraud vulnerability | authority: supreme court ruling | rule: registration mandatory.

General Power of Attorney (GPA) must be registered at SRO to be valid. For best results, always insist on direct sale deed execution by property owner.
