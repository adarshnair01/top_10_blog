Indian capital gains tax rules under Section 112A and 115BBH impose specific tax rates on equity mutual funds, debt funds, real estate, and Virtual Digital Assets (crypto).

Navigating tax edge cases requires understanding grandfathering clauses, indexation benefits, loss set-off rules, and Advance Tax payment schedules.

---

## 10. Equity LTCG Exemption Threshold & Section 112A Mechanics

*Key Specs: Tax Rate: 12.5% | Exemption: ₹1.25 Lakhs / yr | Period: 12 Months*

Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) on listed equities & equity MFs tax-free up to ₹1.25 Lakhs per financial year. Harvest ₹1.25 Lakhs capital gains annually to rebalance portfolio tax-free.

> **Operational Insight:** Gains exceeding ₹1.25 Lakhs taxed at flat 12.5% without indexation. Ensure STT (Securities Transaction Tax) was paid on acquisition and sale.

## 9. Taxation of Debt Mutual Funds Post-2023 Amendment

*Key Specs: Tax Rate: Slabs Rate (As per Income) | Indexation: Removed | Holding: Any Period*

Debt MFs acquired after April 1, 2023 lost indexation benefits completely. Taxed at individual slab rates (up to 30% + surcharge).

> **Operational Insight:** All gains from debt funds classified as Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) regardless of holding period. Use Arbitrage Funds (taxed as equity) for low-risk short-term parking.

## 8. Set-off and Carry Forward Rules for Capital Losses

*Key Specs: LTCG Loss: Set off against LTCG only | STCG Loss: Set off against STCG & LTCG | Carry Forward: 8 Years*

Short-Term Capital Loss (STCL) can be set off against both STCG and LTCG. Unadjusted losses carried forward up to 8 assessment years.

> **Operational Insight:** Long-Term Capital Loss (LTCL) can ONLY be set off against LTCG. File ITR on or before original due date (July 31) to carry forward losses.

## 7. Taxation of Virtual Digital Assets (Crypto & NFTs) under Section 115BBH

*Key Specs: Tax Rate: Flat 30% + Surcharge | TDS: 1% under Sec 194S | Loss Set-off: Strictly Prohibited*

Gains on crypto and NFTs taxed at flat 30% without basic exemption limit benefit. Loss in one crypto pair CANNOT be set off against gain in another crypto pair.

> **Operational Insight:** Zero deduction allowed except cost of acquisition. 1% TDS deducted on all crypto sell transactions on Indian exchanges.

## 6. Taxation of Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) on Maturity vs Secondary Sale

*Key Specs: Maturity Tax: 100% Tax-Free | Secondary Sale Tax: 12.5% LTCG | Period: 3 Years*

Capital gains on SGBs redeemed with RBI upon 8-year maturity are COMPLETELY TAX-FREE. Holding SGBs over 3 years qualifies as LTCG taxed at 12.5%.

> **Operational Insight:** Premature secondary market sales on stock exchange before 8 years attract capital gains tax. Hold SGBs till official RBI maturity for 100% tax exemption.

## 5. REITs and InvITs Distribution Taxation Mechanics

*Key Specs: Components: Dividend, Interest, Repayment of Debt | Tax Treatment: Split Matrix*

REIT payouts split into Dividend, Interest, Rental Income, and Repayment of Debt. Repayment of debt portion taxed as capital gains if exceeds cost of acquisition.

> **Operational Insight:** Dividend tax-free if SPV opted for old tax regime; Interest taxed at slab rate. Check annual tax statement issued by REIT manager before filing ITR.

## 4. Taxation of Employee Stock Options (ESOPs) & RSUs for Tech Workers

*Key Specs: Prerequisite Tax: Exercise Date (Slab Rate) | Capital Gain Tax: Sale Date (STCG/LTCG)*

ESOPs taxed at TWO stages: First as Perquisite (salary slab) on exercise date. DIPP-recognized startups eligible for deferred ESOP tax payment.

> **Operational Insight:** Second as Capital Gains on final sale date based on FMV on exercise date. Keep Form 16 and ESOP exercise statement synchronized.

## 3. Taxation of US Stocks & Foreign Equities for Indian Residents

*Key Specs: LTCG Period: 24 Months | LTCG Rate: 12.5% | Schedule FA: Mandatory Disclosure*

Foreign equities (e.g. US stocks) qualify as LTCG after 24 months holding. Must declare all foreign stock holdings in Schedule FA of ITR.

> **Operational Insight:** LTCG taxed at 12.5% without indexation; STCG taxed at individual slab rates. Claim Foreign Tax Credit (FTC) by filing Form 67 for US dividend withholding tax.

## 2. Capital Gains Tax Exemption via Section 54F (Property Reinvestment)

*Key Specs: Condition: Reinvest Net Sale Consideration | Timeline: 1 yr prior / 2 yrs post sale | Asset: Residential House*

Exempt LTCG from sale of shares/land by investing net sale proceeds in residential house. Capped at maximum reinvestment limit of ₹10 Crores.

> **Operational Insight:** Must buy house within 1 year before or 2 years after asset sale date. Deposit unutilized proceeds in Capital Gains Account Scheme (CGAS) before ITR deadline.

## 1. Advance Tax Schedule & Interest Penalties under Section 234B & 234C

*Key Specs: Threshold: Tax Liability > ₹10,000 | Installments: June (15%), Sept (45%), Dec (75%), March (100%)*

If net tax liability exceeds ₹10,000, must pay Advance Tax in 4 quarterly installments. Capital gains occurring after an installment date can be paid in remaining quarters.

> **Operational Insight:** Failure triggers 1% per month interest penalty under Section 234B and 234C. Pay estimated advance tax immediately in quarter capital gain is realized.
