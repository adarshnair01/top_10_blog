Navigating DGCA aviation security guidelines for domestic flights in India requires knowing exact weight limits, power bank carry-on rules, and prohibited cabin items.

Complying with airport security requires verifying power bank milliampere-hour (mAh) limits, liquid container sizes, and checked baggage dimensions.

---

## 10. Power Bank Carry-On Rules & Wh Capacity Limits

*Key Specs: Status: Hand Luggage ONLY | Prohibition: Strictly Banned in Checked Baggage | Limit: 100Wh (27,000 mAh)*

Power banks, lithium spare batteries, and e-cigarettes are STRICTLY PROHIBITED in checked luggage. Cap limit is 100Wh (approx 27,000 mAh at 3.7V); batteries 100Wh-160Wh require airline approval.

> **Operational Insight:** Must carry power banks inside hand baggage only. Power banks exceeding 160Wh are completely banned on passenger aircraft.

## 9. Liquid, Aerosol & Gel (LAG) Limits for Domestic Cabin Baggage

*Key Specs: Cabin Container Limit: 100 ml per item | Total Limit: 1 Liter Transparent Bag | Checked: Unlimited*

Liquids, gels, lotions, and aerosols in cabin baggage must be in containers of 100 ml or less. Containers larger than 100 ml are confiscated at CISF security screening, even if half empty.

> **Operational Insight:** All 100 ml containers must fit inside a single transparent resealable 1-liter bag. Pack full-sized shampoos, perfume bottles, and sunscreen in checked luggage.

## 8. Domestic Airlines Checked Baggage Allowance & Excess Weight Fees

*Key Specs: Free Weight Limit: 15 kg (Economy) | Hand Baggage Limit: 7 kg | Excess Fee: ₹550/kg*

Standard domestic economy tickets allow 15 kg checked baggage per passenger. Air India offers higher 20-25 kg checked baggage allowance.

> **Operational Insight:** Hand baggage limit is strictly 1 laptop bag/handbag up to 7 kg. Pre-book excess baggage online 6 hours prior to save 50% over airport counter rates.

## 7. Prohibited Items in Cabin Baggage (Sharp Objects, Tools & Sporting Gear)

*Key Specs: Prohibited: Scissors (>6cm), Pocket Knives, Screw Drivers, Cricket Bats | Action: Confiscation*

Sharp metallic objects, Swiss army knives, scissors with blades >6cm are banned in cabin bags. Confiscated items at CISF security gates are permanently destroyed or auctioned.

> **Operational Insight:** Tools (wrenches, pliers, drill bits) and sporting bats/clubs must go into checked baggage. Pack multi-tools, nail clippers, and sporting equipment in checked suitcases.

## 6. Carrying Alcohol & Alcoholic Beverages on Domestic Flights

*Key Specs: Status: Checked Baggage ONLY | Limit: 5 Liters per Passenger | ABV Limit: 24% to 70% ABV*

Alcoholic beverages (24% to 70% ABV) permitted in checked baggage up to 5 liters per passenger. Carrying open alcohol bottles or consuming alcohol on board is a punishable offense.

> **Operational Insight:** Liquors under 24% ABV (beer, wine) have no DGCA quantity limit within checked weight allowance. Pack glass alcohol bottles in bubble wrap inside hardshell checked luggage.

## 5. Carrying Fresh Food, Spices, Pickles & Ghee

*Key Specs: Cabin Status: Ghee & Pickles Banned in Cabin | Checked Status: Permitted in Sealed Container*

Pickles, ghee, oil, and liquid food items strictly prohibited in cabin hand baggage. Dry snacks (sweets, biscuits, dry fruits) allowed in cabin hand baggage.

> **Operational Insight:** Permitted in checked luggage if packed in leak-proof sealed plastic containers. Wrap pickle jars in multiple sealed polythene bags to prevent grease damage.

## 4. Transit Rules for Coconut (Dry Copra) Prohibition

*Key Specs: Status: Strictly Banned in Cabin & Checked Baggage | Reason: Highly Flammable Solid*

Dry coconut (Copra) is classified as a dangerous flammable solid under DGCA rules. Contains high oil content prone to spontaneous combustion under pressure.

> **Operational Insight:** STRICTLY BANNED in both hand cabin baggage AND checked baggage. Fresh green coconuts with water also banned due to liquid volume limits.

## 3. Carrying Firearms, Matchboxes & Lighter Regulations

*Key Specs: Lighters: Strictly Banned | Matchbox: 1 Safety Matchbox on Person | Firearms: License + Security Fee*

Lighters and butane torches are completely banned in both cabin and checked baggage. Licensed firearms/ammunition require 24h prior airline notice, valid license, and ₹5000 handling fee.

> **Operational Insight:** One small safety matchbox permitted on person (pocket), but not inside hand bags. Surrender firearms at airline security counter before main check-in.

## 2. Musical Instruments as Cabin vs Checked Baggage

*Key Specs: Small Instruments: Violin/Flute (Cabin) | Large: Guitar/Cello (Extra Seat / Hardshell)*

Small musical instruments (flute, violin) can replace standard 7 kg hand bag. Loosen instrument strings before flight to prevent bridge damage from cabin pressure drops.

> **Operational Insight:** Guitars require purchasing an extra seat or packing in flight-ready hardshell case for checked transit. Label instrument case fragile at airline check-in counter.

## 1. Carrying Medical Equipment (CPAP, Oxygen Concentrators & Insulin)

*Key Specs: Approval: Doctor Certificate + Airline NOC | CPAP: Allowed Free | Battery: Dry Cell*

CPAP machines, nebulizers, and portable oxygen concentrators (POC) allowed on board free. Insulin pens and needles permitted in cabin with valid doctor prescription.

> **Operational Insight:** Must carry medical certificate from registered doctor explaining equipment need. Inform airline disability customer care desk 48 hours prior to flight date.
