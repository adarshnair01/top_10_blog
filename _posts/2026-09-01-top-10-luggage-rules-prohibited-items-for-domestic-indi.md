Navigating DGCA aviation security guidelines for domestic flights in India requires knowing exact weight limits, power bank carry-on rules, and prohibited cabin items.

Complying with airport security requires verifying power bank milliampere-hour (mAh) limits, liquid container sizes, and checked baggage dimensions.

## 10. Power Bank Carry-On Rules & Wh Capacity Limits

Power banks, lithium spare batteries, and e-cigarettes are STRICTLY PROHIBITED in checked luggage. Cap limit is 100Wh (approx 27,000 mAh at 3.7V); batteries 100Wh-160Wh require airline approval. Key operational metrics show status: hand luggage only | prohibition: strictly banned in checked baggage | limit: 100wh (27,000 mah).

Must carry power banks inside hand baggage only. For best results, power banks exceeding 160Wh are completely banned on passenger aircraft.

## 9. Liquid, Aerosol & Gel (LAG) Limits for Domestic Cabin Baggage

Liquids, gels, lotions, and aerosols in cabin baggage must be in containers of 100 ml or less. Containers larger than 100 ml are confiscated at CISF security screening, even if half empty. Key operational metrics show cabin container limit: 100 ml per item | total limit: 1 liter transparent bag | checked: unlimited.

All 100 ml containers must fit inside a single transparent resealable 1-liter bag. For best results, pack full-sized shampoos, perfume bottles, and sunscreen in checked luggage.

## 8. Domestic Airlines Checked Baggage Allowance & Excess Weight Fees

Standard domestic economy tickets allow 15 kg checked baggage per passenger. Air India offers higher 20-25 kg checked baggage allowance. Key operational metrics show free weight limit: 15 kg (economy) | hand baggage limit: 7 kg | excess fee: ₹550/kg.

Hand baggage limit is strictly 1 laptop bag/handbag up to 7 kg. For best results, pre-book excess baggage online 6 hours prior to save 50% over airport counter rates.

## 7. Prohibited Items in Cabin Baggage (Sharp Objects, Tools & Sporting Gear)

Sharp metallic objects, Swiss army knives, scissors with blades >6cm are banned in cabin bags. Confiscated items at CISF security gates are permanently destroyed or auctioned. Key operational metrics show prohibited: scissors (>6cm), pocket knives, screw drivers, cricket bats | action: confiscation.

Tools (wrenches, pliers, drill bits) and sporting bats/clubs must go into checked baggage. For best results, pack multi-tools, nail clippers, and sporting equipment in checked suitcases.

## 6. Carrying Alcohol & Alcoholic Beverages on Domestic Flights

Alcoholic beverages (24% to 70% ABV) permitted in checked baggage up to 5 liters per passenger. Carrying open alcohol bottles or consuming alcohol on board is a punishable offense. Key operational metrics show status: checked baggage only | limit: 5 liters per passenger | abv limit: 24% to 70% abv.

Liquors under 24% ABV (beer, wine) have no DGCA quantity limit within checked weight allowance. For best results, pack glass alcohol bottles in bubble wrap inside hardshell checked luggage.

## 5. Carrying Fresh Food, Spices, Pickles & Ghee

Pickles, ghee, oil, and liquid food items strictly prohibited in cabin hand baggage. Dry snacks (sweets, biscuits, dry fruits) allowed in cabin hand baggage. Key operational metrics show cabin status: ghee & pickles banned in cabin | checked status: permitted in sealed container.

Permitted in checked luggage if packed in leak-proof sealed plastic containers. For best results, wrap pickle jars in multiple sealed polythene bags to prevent grease damage.

## 4. Transit Rules for Coconut (Dry Copra) Prohibition

Dry coconut (Copra) is classified as a dangerous flammable solid under DGCA rules. Contains high oil content prone to spontaneous combustion under pressure. Key operational metrics show status: strictly banned in cabin & checked baggage | reason: highly flammable solid.

STRICTLY BANNED in both hand cabin baggage AND checked baggage. For best results, fresh green coconuts with water also banned due to liquid volume limits.

## 3. Carrying Firearms, Matchboxes & Lighter Regulations

Lighters and butane torches are completely banned in both cabin and checked baggage. Licensed firearms/ammunition require 24h prior airline notice, valid license, and ₹5000 handling fee. Key operational metrics show lighters: strictly banned | matchbox: 1 safety matchbox on person | firearms: license + security fee.

One small safety matchbox permitted on person (pocket), but not inside hand bags. For best results, surrender firearms at airline security counter before main check-in.

## 2. Musical Instruments as Cabin vs Checked Baggage

Small musical instruments (flute, violin) can replace standard 7 kg hand bag. Loosen instrument strings before flight to prevent bridge damage from cabin pressure drops. Key operational metrics show small instruments: violin/flute (cabin) | large: guitar/cello (extra seat / hardshell).

Guitars require purchasing an extra seat or packing in flight-ready hardshell case for checked transit. For best results, label instrument case fragile at airline check-in counter.

## 1. Carrying Medical Equipment (CPAP, Oxygen Concentrators & Insulin)

CPAP machines, nebulizers, and portable oxygen concentrators (POC) allowed on board free. Insulin pens and needles permitted in cabin with valid doctor prescription. Key operational metrics show approval: doctor certificate + airline noc | cpap: allowed free | battery: dry cell.

Must carry medical certificate from registered doctor explaining equipment need. For best results, inform airline disability customer care desk 48 hours prior to flight date.
