Inoperative bank accounts and unclaimed fixed deposits across Indian banks run into tens of thousands of crores. The RBI UDGAM portal simplifies locating and claiming lost family deposits.

Claiming unclaimed deposits requires verifying bank master lists, submitting legal heir certificates, and filing indemnity bonds with bank branches.

---

## 10. Searching Unclaimed Deposits on RBI UDGAM Portal

*Key Specs: Portal: udgam.rbi.org.in | Coverage: 30+ Major Banks | Input: Name, PAN, DOB*

Register on RBI UDGAM (Unclaimed Deposits - Gateway to Access information) portal. Displays Unclaimed Deposit Reference Number (UDRN) and bank name.

> **Operational Insight:** Search across participating commercial banks using owner name and PAN/Aadhaar. Covers savings accounts, FDs, RDs, and demand drafts unpaid for 10+ years.

## 9. Understanding Deaf (Depositor Education and Awareness Fund)

*Key Specs: Authority: RBI DEAF Cell | Transfer Window: 10 Years | Rule: Principal + Interest Refundable*

Balances unclaimed for 10 years transferred from bank to RBI DEAF fund. RBI refunds principal plus accrued interest through home bank branch.

> **Operational Insight:** Depositors retain full legal right to claim balance at any time. No loss of funds occurs even after transfer to DEAF.

## 8. Submitting Physical Claim Dossier to Home Bank Branch

*Key Specs: Requirement: UDRN Number + KYC | Documents: Aadhaar, PAN, Passbook | Verification: Branch Head*

Take UDRN search printout to nearest branch of specific bank. Branch verifies records against legacy ledger archives.

> **Operational Insight:** Submit claim application along with original passbook/FD receipt and KYC. Process takes 7-14 working days for balance transfer to active account.

## 7. Claiming Unclaimed Accounts Belonging to Deceased Relatives

*Key Specs: Category: Legal Heir Claim | Documents: Death Cert, Legal Heir Cert, Indemnity Bond | Rule: Zero Penalty*

Legal heirs submit death certificate and legal heirship certificate. Two independent guarantors sign indemnity bond at bank branch.

> **Operational Insight:** If claim value exceeds ₹5 Lakhs, bank demands probate or succession certificate. Bank releases funds to joint legal heir account or designated nominee.

## 6. Resolving Name Discrepancies in Legacy Bank Records

*Key Specs: Issue: Spelling Error in Old Records | Solution: Gazetted Affidavit / CA Cert | Action: Manual Verification*

Old physical ledgers often contain spelling variations or missing surnames. Cross-reference old address details with old passport or ration card.

> **Operational Insight:** Submit notarized affidavit confirming single and same person identity. Branch manager verifies legacy signature registers.

## 5. Recovering Unclaimed Fixed Deposits (FDs) and Reinvestments

*Key Specs: Status: Matured Unclaimed FD | Rule: Savings Interest Rate | Claim: Re-issue or Cashout*

Matured FDs left unclaimed earn interest at prevailing savings account rate. If original FD receipt lost, submit Form L-106 indemnity bond.

> **Operational Insight:** Submit original physical FD receipt (FD Certificate) to branch. Choose between instant bank credit or booking fresh term deposit.

## 4. Claiming Unclaimed Dividends & Shares from IEPF Portal

*Key Specs: Portal: iepf.gov.in | Authority: IEPF Authority | Form: Form IEPF-5 | Coverage: Corporate Shares*

Unclaimed company dividends and shares after 7 years transferred to IEPF. Submit physical verification dossier to company Nodal Officer.

> **Operational Insight:** File online Form IEPF-5 on MCA portal with NSDL/CDSL Demat details. IEPF authority transfers shares back to Demat account.

## 3. Tracing Lost Bank Accounts of Pre-CBS Era (Before 2005)

*Key Specs: Challenge: Pre-Digital Paper Ledgers | Method: Archive Ledger Search | Location: Legacy Branch*

Pre-2005 bank accounts were maintained in physical paper ledgers. Branch archivists locate physical ledger folio in bank vault.

> **Operational Insight:** Identify original branch code and submit old account number/passbook. Old account migrated into modern Core Banking System (CBS) for payout.

## 2. Claiming Unclaimed Demand Drafts (DD) & Banker's Cheques

*Key Specs: Validity: 3 Months | Rule: Refund to Buyer Account | Action: Revalidation / Cancellation*

Demand drafts un-encashed after 3 months can only be refunded to purchaser. Unclaimed DDs older than 10 years searchable on UDGAM portal.

> **Operational Insight:** If purchaser lost DD copy, file cancellation application with bank. Money credited back to purchaser's active bank account.

## 1. Tracking Progress of Unclaimed Deposit Claims Online

*Key Specs: Ref: Claim Tracking ID | Service: Bank Nodal Officer | Escalation: Banking Ombudsman*

Collect acknowledgment receipt containing claim reference number from branch. If branch delays processing beyond 30 days, submit complaint to Nodal Officer.

> **Operational Insight:** Track settlement status on bank official unclaimed deposit portal. Escalate to RBI Ombudsman if claim remains unpaid past 45 days.
