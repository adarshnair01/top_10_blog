Managing Employee Provident Fund (EPF) transfers when switching jobs in India requires mastering the Universal Account Number (UAN) digital portal. Resolving employer approval roadblocks guarantees pension continuity.

Navigating EPFO protocols requires referencing Employees' Provident Funds Scheme 1952, Form 13 transfer rules, and EPS pension certificate guidelines.

## 10. How to activate Universal Account Number (UAN) online?

Visit EPFO Member Portal and click 'Activate UAN'. OTP sent to Aadhaar-linked mobile number completes activation. Key operational metrics show portal: epfo member sevottam | requirement: mobile + aadhaar | tat: instant.

Enter UAN, Member ID, Aadhaar number, name, and date of birth. For best results, set strong password and verify email address immediately.

## 9. How to transfer EPF online when switching jobs (Form 13)?

Log in to UAN Member Portal and select 'One Member - One EPF Account (Transfer Request)'. Employer approves digital Form 13 transfer via Digital Signature (DSC). Key operational metrics show portal: unified member portal | feature: one member one epf account | approval: previous/current employer.

Verify previous employment details and select attesting employer (current or previous). For best results, track transfer claim status under 'View Claim Status' tab.

## 8. How to correct Date of Exit on EPF portal if previous employer failed to update?

Employees can self-mark Date of Exit 2 months after leaving previous job. Select reason for leaving (short service, health, permanent departure). Key operational metrics show portal: uan portal | feature: mark exit | limitation: 2 months post leaving.

Select 'Manage' -> 'Mark Exit' on UAN Portal. For best results, date of Exit must fall in the month last PF contribution was remitted.

## 7. How to merge multiple UANs into a single active UAN number?

Employees having multiple UANs must request consolidation under active UAN. Previous UAN balance transferred to current active UAN. Key operational metrics show portal: epfo portal / employer helpdesk | process: uan consolidation | protection: prevents block.

Mail EPFO at uanepf@epfindia.gov.in or report to current employer. For best results, only one UAN per individual is legally permitted under EPFO rules.

## 6. How to withdraw EPF balance online for medical, house purchase, or marriage advance?

Submit Form 31 online for non-refundable partial PF advance. Money credited directly to bank account linked with Aadhaar. Key operational metrics show portal: uan portal | form: form 31 (partial withdrawal) | tat: 3-5 working days.

Eligible for house purchase (5 yrs service), illness, or marriage (7 yrs service). For best results, no physical documents required for online partial withdrawal.

## 5. How to claim EPS Pension Scheme certificate (Form 10C)?

Form 10C issued for pension withdrawal or scheme certificate. Mandatory if total service is under 10 years and employee leaves workforce. Key operational metrics show portal: epfo portal | form: form 10c | condition: under 10 years service.

Scheme Certificate retains pension service record for future employment. For best results, continuous 10 years service unlocks monthly pension eligibility at age 58.

## 4. How to resolve 'Name / Father Name Mismatch' blocking EPF claim?

Submit online Joint Declaration request on UAN portal. Attach Aadhaar, PAN card, and school leaving certificate copy. Key operational metrics show portal: joint declaration portal | approval: employer & field office | document: aadhaar / pan.

Employer signs request digitally and forwards to regional EPFO field office. For best results, field officer updates master EPFO database within 7-14 days.

## 3. How to file an EPF Grievance on EPFiGMS portal for delayed claims?

Log onto epfigms.gov.in and register complaint under UAN number. Regional PF Commissioner reviews and resolves grievance within 15 days. Key operational metrics show portal: epfigms portal | resolution window: 15 days | escalation: regional pf commissioner.

Upload claim acknowledgement receipt and bank statement copy. For best results, track grievance registration number online for real-time status.

## 2. How to check EPF Passbook balance via SMS and Missed Call?

Give missed call to 9966044425 from registered mobile number. Provides instant breakdown of employee, employer, and pension contributions. Key operational metrics show number: 9966044425 (missed call) / 7738299899 (sms) | requirement: activated uan.

SMS format 'EPFOHO UAN ENG' sent to 7738299899 returns balance. For best results, requires UAN to be linked with Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account.

## 1. How to update bank account details (KYC) on UAN portal?

Log in and navigate to 'Manage' -> 'KYC' -> Select 'Bank'. Employer approves pending bank KYC using Digital Signature Certificate. Key operational metrics show portal: member portal | section: manage kyc | approval: employer dsc.

Enter bank account number and IFSC code; system validates via bank API. Ensure bank account name matches UAN profile name exactly.
