Bank account liens and debit freezes triggered by Cyber Cell notices or re-KYC non-compliance cause severe financial disruption for Indian account holders.

Resolving bank liens requires interfacing with investigating officers, submitting KYC verification documents, and filing RBI Ombudsman complaints.

## 10. Cyber Cell Notice Account Lien Clearance Protocol

Police place lien on account due to suspect P2P or crypto transaction chain. Submit bank statements and invoice proofs to IO to obtain NOC. Key operational metrics show source: state cyber police | action: contact io | requirement: proof of legitimate income.

Request bank for Cyber Police Station name, Crime No, and IO phone number. For best results, bank removes lien upon receiving official NOC email from Cyber Cell.

## 9. Re-KYC Account Debit Block Resolution

Banks block debit transactions if periodic re-KYC is not updated. Visit home branch with Aadhaar and PAN card for physical re-KYC. Key operational metrics show mandate: rbi master direction | window: every 2-10 years | option: video kyc / netbanking.

Submit self-declaration online if address and details remain unchanged. For best results, video KYC (V-KYC) facility enables remote re-verification.

## 8. Inoperative / Dormant Account Activation Process

Accounts with no transaction for 2 years classified as dormant. Perform single micro-deposit transaction to reactivate account. Key operational metrics show condition: no customer txn for 2 years | rule: zero activation fee | tat: 24 hours.

Submit fresh KYC documents and passbook copy to home branch. For best results, banks prohibited from charging any fee for activating dormant accounts.

## 7. Resolving Post-Death Deceased Account Claim Procedures

Nominee submits death certificate and ID proof for swift balance transfer. Claims up to ₹5 Lakhs settled without probate or succession certificate. Key operational metrics show limit: up to ₹5 lakhs without probate | form: claim form 14 | timeline: 15 days.

Without nomination, legal heirs submit indemnity bond and survivor certificate. Ensure valid nominee is updated in all savings and FD accounts.

## 6. Unfreezing Accounts Blocked Due to PAN-Aadhaar Non-Linking

Unlinked PAN card renders bank account non-compliant under IT rules. Link PAN with Aadhaar on IT portal and inform bank branch. Key operational metrics show status: inoperative pan | rule: higher tds deduction | remedy: link pan & pay penalty.

Bank deducts TDS at higher 20% rate on interest income. For best results, update bank record once PAN status shows active on e-Filing portal.

## 5. High-Value Foreign Inward Remittance (FIRC) Hold Resolution

Banks hold incoming international wire transfers until Purpose Code is declared. Bank issues e-FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate) via EDPMS. Key operational metrics show regulation: fema compliance | document: firc / purpose code | action: submit purpose declaration.

Select correct Purpose Code (e.g. P0802 for software export). For best results, essential document for GST zero-rated export tax compliance.

## 4. Major / Minor Account Conversion upon Turning 18

Account frozen when minor account holder turns 18 years old. Guardian's joint operational authority terminated upon conversion. Key operational metrics show requirement: fresh kyc & signature update | authority: rbi deposit rules | form: minor to major.

Submit fresh PAN, Aadhaar, and new specimen signature at branch. For best results, issues fresh cheque book and debit card in account holder's name.

## 3. Resolving Accounts Frozen Due to Court Garnishee Orders

Court attaches bank account balance to recover judicial decree debts. Excess balance above court order remains fully operational. Key operational metrics show authority: civil court order | scope: specified debt amount | legal remedies.

Lien applies ONLY up to exact amount specified in court order. For best results, engage legal counsel to challenge or deposit disputed amount in court.

## 2. Clearing Fraudulent Account Block Flagged by CIBIL / CEX

Wrong identity match flags bank account under fraud monitoring radar. Bank conducts manual audit and issues clearance certificate. Key operational metrics show source: credit bureau fraud flag | action: dispute resolution | tat: 30 days.

File dispute ticket on CIBIL or Experian portal with valid identity proof. For best results, monitor annual free credit reports to catch identity mis-matches early.

## 1. RBI Ombudsman Escalation for Delayed Lien Removal

If bank fails to remove invalid lien within 30 days of submitting NOC, alert RBI. RBI directs bank to remove lien and pay compensation for wrongful freeze. Key operational metrics show portal: rbi cms | rule: 30-day resolution limit | penalty: bank compensation.

File online complaint on RBI CMS portal citing loss of access. For best results, attach copy of Cyber Cell NOC and written bank branch receipts.
