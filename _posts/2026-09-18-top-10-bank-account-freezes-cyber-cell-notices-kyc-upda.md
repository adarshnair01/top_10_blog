Bank account liens and debit freezes triggered by Cyber Cell notices or re-KYC non-compliance cause severe financial disruption for Indian account holders.

Resolving bank liens requires interfacing with investigating officers, submitting KYC verification documents, and filing RBI Ombudsman complaints.

---

## 10. Cyber Cell Notice Account Lien Clearance Protocol

*Key Specs: Source: State Cyber Police | Action: Contact IO | Requirement: Proof of Legitimate Income*

Police place lien on account due to suspect P2P or crypto transaction chain. Submit bank statements and invoice proofs to IO to obtain NOC.

> **Operational Insight:** Request bank for Cyber Police Station name, Crime No, and IO phone number. Bank removes lien upon receiving official NOC email from Cyber Cell.

## 9. Re-KYC Account Debit Block Resolution

*Key Specs: Mandate: RBI Master Direction | Window: Every 2-10 Years | Option: Video KYC / Netbanking*

Banks block debit transactions if periodic re-KYC is not updated. Visit home branch with Aadhaar and PAN card for physical re-KYC.

> **Operational Insight:** Submit self-declaration online if address and details remain unchanged. Video KYC (V-KYC) facility enables remote re-verification.

## 8. Inoperative / Dormant Account Activation Process

*Key Specs: Condition: No Customer Txn for 2 Years | Rule: Zero Activation Fee | TAT: 24 Hours*

Accounts with no transaction for 2 years classified as dormant. Perform single micro-deposit transaction to reactivate account.

> **Operational Insight:** Submit fresh KYC documents and passbook copy to home branch. Banks prohibited from charging any fee for activating dormant accounts.

## 7. Resolving Post-Death Deceased Account Claim Procedures

*Key Specs: Limit: Up to ₹5 Lakhs without Probate | Form: Claim Form 14 | Timeline: 15 Days*

Nominee submits death certificate and ID proof for swift balance transfer. Claims up to ₹5 Lakhs settled without probate or succession certificate.

> **Operational Insight:** Without nomination, legal heirs submit indemnity bond and survivor certificate. Ensure valid nominee is updated in all savings and FD accounts.

## 6. Unfreezing Accounts Blocked Due to PAN-Aadhaar Non-Linking

*Key Specs: Status: Inoperative PAN | Rule: Higher TDS Deduction | Remedy: Link PAN & Pay Penalty*

Unlinked PAN card renders bank account non-compliant under IT rules. Link PAN with Aadhaar on IT portal and inform bank branch.

> **Operational Insight:** Bank deducts TDS at higher 20% rate on interest income. Update bank record once PAN status shows active on e-Filing portal.

## 5. High-Value Foreign Inward Remittance (FIRC) Hold Resolution

*Key Specs: Regulation: FEMA Compliance | Document: FIRC / Purpose Code | Action: Submit Purpose Declaration*

Banks hold incoming international wire transfers until Purpose Code is declared. Bank issues e-FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate) via EDPMS.

> **Operational Insight:** Select correct Purpose Code (e.g. P0802 for software export). Essential document for GST zero-rated export tax compliance.

## 4. Major / Minor Account Conversion upon Turning 18

*Key Specs: Requirement: Fresh KYC & Signature Update | Authority: RBI Deposit Rules | Form: Minor to Major*

Account frozen when minor account holder turns 18 years old. Guardian's joint operational authority terminated upon conversion.

> **Operational Insight:** Submit fresh PAN, Aadhaar, and new specimen signature at branch. Issues fresh cheque book and debit card in account holder's name.

## 3. Resolving Accounts Frozen Due to Court Garnishee Orders

*Key Specs: Authority: Civil Court Order | Scope: Specified Debt Amount | Legal Remedies*

Court attaches bank account balance to recover judicial decree debts. Excess balance above court order remains fully operational.

> **Operational Insight:** Lien applies ONLY up to exact amount specified in court order. Engage legal counsel to challenge or deposit disputed amount in court.

## 2. Clearing Fraudulent Account Block Flagged by CIBIL / CEX

*Key Specs: Source: Credit Bureau Fraud Flag | Action: Dispute Resolution | TAT: 30 Days*

Wrong identity match flags bank account under fraud monitoring radar. Bank conducts manual audit and issues clearance certificate.

> **Operational Insight:** File dispute ticket on CIBIL or Experian portal with valid identity proof. Monitor annual free credit reports to catch identity mis-matches early.

## 1. RBI Ombudsman Escalation for Delayed Lien Removal

*Key Specs: Portal: RBI CMS | Rule: 30-Day Resolution Limit | Penalty: Bank Compensation*

If bank fails to remove invalid lien within 30 days of submitting NOC, alert RBI. RBI directs bank to remove lien and pay compensation for wrongful freeze.

> **Operational Insight:** File online complaint on RBI CMS portal citing loss of access. Attach copy of Cyber Cell NOC and written bank branch receipts.
