Traveling abroad from India with traditional credit cards incurs high 3.5% foreign exchange markup fees plus GST. Zero forex markup credit cards eliminate international markup fees completely.

Selecting zero forex cards involves comparing markup fees, ATM withdrawal charges, TCS tax implications, and lounge access benefits worldwide.

## 2. Understanding TCS (Tax Collected at Source) on International Credit Card Spends

*Key Specs: Rule: LRS Ceiling | Threshold: ₹7 Lakhs | TCS Rate: 20% Above Threshold*

International credit card spends are exempt from LRS TCS up to ₹7 Lakhs per year. TCS is NOT a tax cost; claim 100% refund while filing annual income tax return.

> **Operational Insight:** Spends exceeding ₹7 Lakhs attract 20% TCS (5% for education/medical). Track cumulative annual foreign spends to manage TCS cash flow.

## 1. International ATM Cash Withdrawal Playbook for Indian Cards

*Key Specs: Fee Structure: Flat ₹250-500 + ATM Operator Fee | Advice: Minimize Cash Withdrawals*

Withdrawing cash abroad incurs flat bank ATM fees plus local ATM operator charges. Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) charges hidden 5-8% extra conversion fee.

> **Operational Insight:** Always select 'Debit in Local Currency' (NOT INR) at overseas ATMs to avoid DCC markup. Carry a mix of zero forex cards and minimal local cash currency.
