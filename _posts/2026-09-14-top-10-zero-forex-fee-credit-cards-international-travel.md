## 2. Understanding TCS (Tax Collected at Source) on International Credit Card Spends

International credit card spends are exempt from LRS TCS up to ₹7 Lakhs per year. TCS is NOT a tax cost; claim 100% refund while filing annual income tax return. Key operational metrics show rule: lrs ceiling | threshold: ₹7 lakhs | tcs rate: 20% above threshold.

Spends exceeding ₹7 Lakhs attract 20% TCS (5% for education/medical). For best results, track cumulative annual foreign spends to manage TCS cash flow.

## 1. International ATM Cash Withdrawal Playbook for Indian Cards

Withdrawing cash abroad incurs flat bank ATM fees plus local ATM operator charges. Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) charges hidden 5-8% extra conversion fee. Key operational metrics show fee structure: flat ₹250-500 + atm operator fee | advice: minimize cash withdrawals.

Always select 'Debit in Local Currency' (NOT INR) at overseas ATMs to avoid DCC markup. For best results, carry a mix of zero forex cards and minimal local cash currency.
