# Top 10 'What Happens If...' Flight Delays, Cancellations & Airport Baggage Claims in India

Air travel across Indian domestic sectors can be disrupted by fog seasonal delays, technical faults, and baggage mishandling. Knowing passenger rights under DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements (CAR) guarantees meal vouchers, hotel stays, and financial compensation.

![Top 10 'What Happens If...' Flight Delays, Cancellations & Airport Baggage Claims in India](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1508873696983-2df515122519?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

Evaluating airline rights requires referencing DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M provisions, airline customer service commitments, and airport operator rules.

## 10. What happens if your domestic flight is delayed by more than 2 hours?

**Core Specs & Mechanics:** Rule: Free Refreshments | Authority: DGCA CAR Section 3 | Option: Meals & Drinks

Airline must provide free meals and refreshments for delays over 2 hours (Block 1).

> **Official Rule / Fact:** Delay over 6 hours requires airline to offer alternate flight or 100% refund.

> **Key Context:** Passengers entitled to full communication facilities at airport.

> **Practical Tip:** Collect refreshment voucher at airline customer service desk near gate.

## 9. What happens if an airline cancels your flight within 24 hours of departure?

**Core Specs & Mechanics:** Rule: Alternate Flight or Full Refund + Compensation | Authority: DGCA CAR | Value: Up to ₹10,000

Airline must offer alternate flight or 100% refund.

> **Official Rule / Fact:** Additionally pay compensation up to ₹10,000 based on flight duration.

> **Key Context:** Exempted if cancellation is caused by extraordinary weather/ATC events.

> **Practical Tip:** Demand written reason for cancellation from airline ground staff.

## 8. What happens if an airline denies boarding due to overbooking?

**Core Specs & Mechanics:** Rule: Alternate Flight + 200% Compensation | Authority: DGCA CAR | Limit: Up to ₹20,000

If alternate flight provided within 1 hour: zero compensation.

> **Official Rule / Fact:** If alternate flight beyond 1 hour: airline pays 200% of basic fare + fuel surcharge.

> **Key Context:** Passengers choose between monetary refund or alternate transport.

> **Practical Tip:** Never voluntarily give up seat without written compensation voucher.

## 7. What happens if your checked baggage is lost or delayed by domestic airlines?

**Core Specs & Mechanics:** Rule: PIR Registration & Daily Expense Allowance | Authority: DGCA & Carriage by Air Act | Compensation: Up to ₹20,000

File Property Irregularity Report (PIR) before leaving arrival hall.

> **Official Rule / Fact:** Airline pays interim emergency allowance for purchase of essentials.

> **Key Context:** Baggage unrecovered after 21 days treated as lost; compensation up to ₹20,000.

> **Practical Tip:** Keep baggage tag receipts stuck to boarding pass safe.

## 6. What happens if your luggage is damaged during transit?

**Core Specs & Mechanics:** Rule: Repair or Replacement Claim | Authority: Airline Baggage Policy | Deadline: 7 Days

Report damaged bag to airline baggage counter before exiting airport.

> **Official Rule / Fact:** File written claim within 7 days of receiving damaged luggage.

> **Key Context:** Airline reimburses repair costs or provides replacement suitcase.

> **Practical Tip:** Take clear photo of damaged bag at baggage carousel immediately.

## 5. What happens if you miss your flight due to long airport security queues?

**Core Specs & Mechanics:** Rule: Passenger Responsibility | Authority: Airline Conditions of Carriage | Fee: No-Show Fee

Airlines not legally liable for delays caused by CISF security queues.

> **Official Rule / Fact:** Must pay no-show fee or rebooking difference for next available flight.

> **Key Context:** Some airlines offer gate assistance if requested early in check-in hall.

> **Practical Tip:** Arrive 3 hours prior for domestic flights during peak holiday seasons.

## 4. What happens if you leave a personal item (laptop/phone) on the airplane?

**Core Specs & Mechanics:** Rule: Lost & Found Retrieval | Authority: Airport Lost & Found / CISF | Verification: Serial No & ID

Contact airline ground office or airport Lost & Found portal immediately.

> **Official Rule / Fact:** Items handed over to CISF security custody after aircraft search.

> **Key Context:** Retrieved upon showing valid government ID and purchase invoice.

> **Practical Tip:** File online Lost & Found query on airport official website.

## 3. What happens if weather conditions force a flight diversion to another city?

**Core Specs & Mechanics:** Rule: Duty of Care & Transit | Authority: DGCA Regulations | Duty: Hotel & Food

Airline must provide ground transportation or hotel accommodation at diverted airport.

> **Official Rule / Fact:** Flights resume once destination weather permits.

> **Key Context:** Passengers can disembark at diverted airport if customs/security allows.

> **Practical Tip:** Follow airline ground staff instructions for hotel transfers.

## 2. What happens if you need to cancel a flight ticket within 24 hours of booking?

**Core Specs & Mechanics:** Rule: Zero Cancellation Fee Window | Authority: DGCA CAR 24-Hour Rule | Condition: Flight 7 Days Away

Can cancel or amend ticket within 24 hours of booking without any penalty.

> **Official Rule / Fact:** Applies if booking was made at least 7 days prior to flight departure.

> **Key Context:** Full refund processed back to original payment method.

> **Practical Tip:** Book directly on airline website for seamless 24-hour zero-fee cancellation.

## 1. What happens if medical emergency prevents you from traveling on booked date?

**Core Specs & Mechanics:** Rule: Credit Shell / Date Change Fee Waiver | Authority: Airline Compassionate Policy | Requirement: Medical Certificate

Submit certified medical certificate from registered doctor to airline customer care.

> **Official Rule / Fact:** Airlines grant fee-waiver date change or issue credit shell valid for 1 year.

> **Key Context:** Policy varies by carrier; low-cost airlines require advance notification.

> **Practical Tip:** Email medical documents to airline customer support before flight departure.
