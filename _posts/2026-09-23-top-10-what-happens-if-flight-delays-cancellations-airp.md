Air travel across Indian domestic sectors can be disrupted by fog seasonal delays, technical faults, and baggage mishandling. Knowing passenger rights under DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements (CAR) guarantees meal vouchers, hotel stays, and financial compensation.

Evaluating airline rights requires referencing DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M provisions, airline customer service commitments, and airport operator rules.

## 10. What happens if your domestic flight is delayed by more than 2 hours?

Airline must provide free meals and refreshments for delays over 2 hours (Block 1). Passengers entitled to full communication facilities at airport. Key operational metrics show rule: free refreshments | authority: dgca car section 3 | option: meals & drinks.

Delay over 6 hours requires airline to offer alternate flight or 100% refund. For best results, collect refreshment voucher at airline customer service desk near gate.

## 9. What happens if an airline cancels your flight within 24 hours of departure?

Airline must offer alternate flight or 100% refund. Exempted if cancellation is caused by extraordinary weather/ATC events. Key operational metrics show rule: alternate flight or full refund + compensation | authority: dgca car | value: up to ₹10,000.

Additionally pay compensation up to ₹10,000 based on flight duration. For best results, demand written reason for cancellation from airline ground staff.

## 8. What happens if an airline denies boarding due to overbooking?

If alternate flight provided within 1 hour: zero compensation. Passengers choose between monetary refund or alternate transport. Key operational metrics show rule: alternate flight + 200% compensation | authority: dgca car | limit: up to ₹20,000.

If alternate flight beyond 1 hour: airline pays 200% of basic fare + fuel surcharge. For best results, never voluntarily give up seat without written compensation voucher.

## 7. What happens if your checked baggage is lost or delayed by domestic airlines?

File Property Irregularity Report (PIR) before leaving arrival hall. Baggage unrecovered after 21 days treated as lost; compensation up to ₹20,000. Key operational metrics show rule: pir registration & daily expense allowance | authority: dgca & carriage by air act | compensation: up to ₹20,000.

Airline pays interim emergency allowance for purchase of essentials. Keep baggage tag receipts stuck to boarding pass safe.

## 6. What happens if your luggage is damaged during transit?

Report damaged bag to airline baggage counter before exiting airport. Airline reimburses repair costs or provides replacement suitcase. Key operational metrics show rule: repair or replacement claim | authority: airline baggage policy | deadline: 7 days.

File written claim within 7 days of receiving damaged luggage. For best results, take clear photo of damaged bag at baggage carousel immediately.

## 5. What happens if you miss your flight due to long airport security queues?

Airlines not legally liable for delays caused by CISF security queues. Some airlines offer gate assistance if requested early in check-in hall. Key operational metrics show rule: passenger responsibility | authority: airline conditions of carriage | fee: no-show fee.

Must pay no-show fee or rebooking difference for next available flight. For best results, arrive 3 hours prior for domestic flights during peak holiday seasons.

## 4. What happens if you leave a personal item (laptop/phone) on the airplane?

Contact airline ground office or airport Lost & Found portal immediately. Retrieved upon showing valid government ID and purchase invoice. Key operational metrics show rule: lost & found retrieval | authority: airport lost & found / cisf | verification: serial no & id.

Items handed over to CISF security custody after aircraft search. For best results, file online Lost & Found query on airport official website.

## 3. What happens if weather conditions force a flight diversion to another city?

Airline must provide ground transportation or hotel accommodation at diverted airport. Passengers can disembark at diverted airport if customs/security allows. Key operational metrics show rule: duty of care & transit | authority: dgca regulations | duty: hotel & food.

Flights resume once destination weather permits. For best results, follow airline ground staff instructions for hotel transfers.

## 2. What happens if you need to cancel a flight ticket within 24 hours of booking?

Can cancel or amend ticket within 24 hours of booking without any penalty. Full refund processed back to original payment method. Key operational metrics show rule: zero cancellation fee window | authority: dgca car 24-hour rule | condition: flight 7 days away.

Applies if booking was made at least 7 days prior to flight departure. Book directly on airline website for seamless 24-hour zero-fee cancellation.

## 1. What happens if medical emergency prevents you from traveling on booked date?

Submit certified medical certificate from registered doctor to airline customer care. Policy varies by carrier; low-cost airlines require advance notification. Key operational metrics show rule: credit shell / date change fee waiver | authority: airline compassionate policy | requirement: medical certificate.

Airlines grant fee-waiver date change or issue credit shell valid for 1 year. For best results, email medical documents to airline customer support before flight departure.
