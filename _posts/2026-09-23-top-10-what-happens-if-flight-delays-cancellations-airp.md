Air travel across Indian domestic sectors can be disrupted by fog seasonal delays, technical faults, and baggage mishandling. Knowing passenger rights under DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements (CAR) guarantees meal vouchers, hotel stays, and financial compensation.

Evaluating airline rights requires referencing DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M provisions, airline customer service commitments, and airport operator rules.

---

## 10. What happens if your domestic flight is delayed by more than 2 hours?

*Key Specs: Rule: Free Refreshments | Authority: DGCA CAR Section 3 | Option: Meals & Drinks*

Airline must provide free meals and refreshments for delays over 2 hours (Block 1). Passengers entitled to full communication facilities at airport.

> **Operational Insight:** Delay over 6 hours requires airline to offer alternate flight or 100% refund. Collect refreshment voucher at airline customer service desk near gate.

## 9. What happens if an airline cancels your flight within 24 hours of departure?

*Key Specs: Rule: Alternate Flight or Full Refund + Compensation | Authority: DGCA CAR | Value: Up to ₹10,000*

Airline must offer alternate flight or 100% refund. Exempted if cancellation is caused by extraordinary weather/ATC events.

> **Operational Insight:** Additionally pay compensation up to ₹10,000 based on flight duration. Demand written reason for cancellation from airline ground staff.

## 8. What happens if an airline denies boarding due to overbooking?

*Key Specs: Rule: Alternate Flight + 200% Compensation | Authority: DGCA CAR | Limit: Up to ₹20,000*

If alternate flight provided within 1 hour: zero compensation. Passengers choose between monetary refund or alternate transport.

> **Operational Insight:** If alternate flight beyond 1 hour: airline pays 200% of basic fare + fuel surcharge. Never voluntarily give up seat without written compensation voucher.

## 7. What happens if your checked baggage is lost or delayed by domestic airlines?

*Key Specs: Rule: PIR Registration & Daily Expense Allowance | Authority: DGCA & Carriage by Air Act | Compensation: Up to ₹20,000*

File Property Irregularity Report (PIR) before leaving arrival hall. Baggage unrecovered after 21 days treated as lost; compensation up to ₹20,000.

> **Operational Insight:** Airline pays interim emergency allowance for purchase of essentials. Keep baggage tag receipts stuck to boarding pass safe.

## 6. What happens if your luggage is damaged during transit?

*Key Specs: Rule: Repair or Replacement Claim | Authority: Airline Baggage Policy | Deadline: 7 Days*

Report damaged bag to airline baggage counter before exiting airport. Airline reimburses repair costs or provides replacement suitcase.

> **Operational Insight:** File written claim within 7 days of receiving damaged luggage. Take clear photo of damaged bag at baggage carousel immediately.

## 5. What happens if you miss your flight due to long airport security queues?

*Key Specs: Rule: Passenger Responsibility | Authority: Airline Conditions of Carriage | Fee: No-Show Fee*

Airlines not legally liable for delays caused by CISF security queues. Some airlines offer gate assistance if requested early in check-in hall.

> **Operational Insight:** Must pay no-show fee or rebooking difference for next available flight. Arrive 3 hours prior for domestic flights during peak holiday seasons.

## 4. What happens if you leave a personal item (laptop/phone) on the airplane?

*Key Specs: Rule: Lost & Found Retrieval | Authority: Airport Lost & Found / CISF | Verification: Serial No & ID*

Contact airline ground office or airport Lost & Found portal immediately. Retrieved upon showing valid government ID and purchase invoice.

> **Operational Insight:** Items handed over to CISF security custody after aircraft search. File online Lost & Found query on airport official website.

## 3. What happens if weather conditions force a flight diversion to another city?

*Key Specs: Rule: Duty of Care & Transit | Authority: DGCA Regulations | Duty: Hotel & Food*

Airline must provide ground transportation or hotel accommodation at diverted airport. Passengers can disembark at diverted airport if customs/security allows.

> **Operational Insight:** Flights resume once destination weather permits. Follow airline ground staff instructions for hotel transfers.

## 2. What happens if you need to cancel a flight ticket within 24 hours of booking?

*Key Specs: Rule: Zero Cancellation Fee Window | Authority: DGCA CAR 24-Hour Rule | Condition: Flight 7 Days Away*

Can cancel or amend ticket within 24 hours of booking without any penalty. Full refund processed back to original payment method.

> **Operational Insight:** Applies if booking was made at least 7 days prior to flight departure. Book directly on airline website for seamless 24-hour zero-fee cancellation.

## 1. What happens if medical emergency prevents you from traveling on booked date?

*Key Specs: Rule: Credit Shell / Date Change Fee Waiver | Authority: Airline Compassionate Policy | Requirement: Medical Certificate*

Submit certified medical certificate from registered doctor to airline customer care. Policy varies by carrier; low-cost airlines require advance notification.

> **Operational Insight:** Airlines grant fee-waiver date change or issue credit shell valid for 1 year. Email medical documents to airline customer support before flight departure.
