Indian Railways operates one of the world's largest rail networks, carrying over 22 million passengers daily. Yet when operational glitches occur—from losing your physical ticket to missing a connecting train—passengers are caught off guard.

Navigating Indian Railways rules requires understanding the IRCTC TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) refund process, Commercial Railway Code guidelines, and station master emergency powers.

## 10. What happens if you lose your physical confirmed train ticket before boarding?

Report to Chief Reservation Supervisor before chart preparation with photo ID. Full refund granted if original ticket is found before train departure. Key operational metrics show rule: duplicate ticket issuance | authority: indian railways commercial code | fee: ₹50-100.

Duplicate ticket issued upon paying ₹50 (Sleeper) or ₹100 (AC class). For best results, carry digital screenshot of booking SMS as instant proof.

## 9. What happens if your train is delayed by more than 3 hours?

Eligible for 100% fare refund if train is delayed over 3 hours at starting station. No cancellation charges deducted for severe railway delay. Key operational metrics show rule: full refund without penalty | authority: irctc tdr rules | condition: ticket not traveled.

Must file TDR online before actual departure of train. For best results, file TDR via IRCTC app before train leaves your boarding station.

## 8. What happens if you miss your train due to a delayed connecting train?

If passenger misses connecting train due to late running of first train, full refund is granted. Filing TDR at junction station enables free booking on next available train. Key operational metrics show rule: alternative accommodation / full refund | authority: combination ticket rules | condition: pnr linked.

Both PNRs must be linked at time of booking. For best results, always link multi-leg train bookings under single journey profile.

## 7. What happens if your confirmed RAC ticket does not get berth allocation?

RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) guarantees seating access on train. If no berth opens up, passenger can travel seated legally. Key operational metrics show rule: seating berth granted | authority: irctc berth matrix | right: half-berth sharing.

TTE must allocate full sleeping berth if confirmed passengers don't show up. For best results, check live berth vacancy on TTE handheld terminal during journey.

## 6. What happens if you board a train with a Waitlisted e-Ticket?

Fully waitlisted e-tickets are automatically cancelled after chart preparation. TTE will charge full fare from origin plus ₹250 penalty. Key operational metrics show rule: strictly illegal | authority: railway act section 137 | penalty: treated as ticketless.

Boarding train with cancelled e-ticket leads to ticketless passenger penalty. For best results, cancel waitlisted ticket and book Tatkaal or Tatkaal Special.

## 5. What happens if you fall ill during a long-distance train journey?

Inform TTE or dial 139 instantly for medical emergency. Emergency medicine administered on board for nominal charge. Key operational metrics show rule: on-board medical assistance | authority: railway medical services | cost: nominal doctor fee.

Railway doctor dispatched to meet train at next major halt station. Keep RailMadad app installed for priority emergency alerts.

## 4. What happens if your luggage is stolen on an Indian Railways train?

Inform TTE immediately; conductor carries pre-printed GRP FIR forms. FIR transferred automatically to jurisdiction railway police station. Key operational metrics show rule: grp fir registration | authority: government railway police (grp) | action: conductor fir form.

No need to disembark train to file police complaint. For best results, secure all luggage with steel cables and padlocks under lower berth.

## 3. What happens if you accidentally drop your phone on railway tracks?

Note exact OHE (Overhead Equipment) pole number written on side track posts. RPF personnel track pole location and retrieve dropped item. Key operational metrics show rule: pole number note | authority: rpf patrol | retrieval: next station stop.

Inform Railway Protection Force (RPF) at next station halt. For best results, never attempt to jump off moving train to collect fallen items.

## 2. What happens if you get down at a station and the train leaves without you?

Inform Station Master immediately; they alert TTE on board moving train. Station Master issues travel permit to catch up on following express. Key operational metrics show rule: luggage protection by tte | authority: station master emergency | next action: board next express.

TTE locks your berth area and hands over luggage at next major junction. Keep phone, wallet, and ID on your person during quick platform walks.

## 1. What happens if you pull the Emergency Alarm Chain (ACP) without valid reason?

Chain pulling permitted ONLY for medical emergency, safety threat, or passenger separation. RPF traces specific coach via mechanical signal indicator. Key operational metrics show rule: criminal offense | authority: railway act section 141 | penalty: ₹1,000 fine / 1 yr jail.

Unjustified chain pulling causes severe corridor train delays. For best results, use chain pulling strictly as absolute last resort for life safety.
