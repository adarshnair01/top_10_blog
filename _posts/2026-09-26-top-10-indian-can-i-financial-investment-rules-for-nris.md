Financial regulations under RBI, FEMA, and SEBI govern how Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Indian residents manage bank accounts, stock investments, mutual funds, and overseas money transfers.

Evaluating financial queries requires consulting Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) provisions, Income Tax Act sections, and RBI circulars.

## 10. Can an NRI continue holding resident Indian bank accounts?

Holding resident bank accounts after acquiring NRI status violates FEMA. Penalty includes fines up to 3x the un-designated amount. Key operational metrics show legal status: strictly illegal | authority: fema section 3 | action: convert to nre/nro.

Must re-designate existing accounts to NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) accounts. For best results, notify bank within 3 months of changing residency status.

## 9. Can I transfer money from NRO account to foreign bank account?

NRIs can repatriate up to USD 1 Million per financial year from NRO account. NRE account funds are fully repatriable without any dollar limits. Key operational metrics show legal status: permitted up to $1m/yr | authority: rbi fema guidelines | requirement: form 15ca/15cb.

Requires CA certificate (Form 15CB) confirming all Indian taxes are paid. For best results, obtain Form 15CB from registered Indian CA before wire transfer.

## 8. Can an Indian resident send money abroad for buying foreign stocks?

Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) permits residents to send up to USD 250,000 annually. TCS (Tax Collected at Source) of 20% applies on remittances exceeding ₹7 Lakhs. Key operational metrics show legal status: permitted up to $250k/yr | authority: rbi lrs scheme | tax: tcs applies.

Used for overseas stock investments, real estate, education, and travel. For best results, claim TCS credit while filing annual Indian income tax return.

## 7. Can an NRI invest in Indian stock market directly?

NRIs can trade Indian equities through Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) or Non-PIS accounts. Intraday trading is strictly prohibited for NRIs; only delivery trades allowed. Key operational metrics show legal status: permitted | authority: sebi & rbi | route: pis / non-pis account.

Must open NRE/NRO Demat and Trading account with SEBI-registered broker. For best results, use Non-PIS NRO account for trading derivative contracts.

## 6. Can I gift money to foreign relatives tax-free under Indian Income Tax Act?

Gifts to specified linear relatives (parents, children, siblings) are tax-free. Non-relatives receiving gifts over ₹50,000 in India face income tax. Key operational metrics show legal status: tax-free for specified relatives | authority: income tax act section 56(2)(x) | limit: lrs ceiling.

Remittance subject to TCS under LRS rules if sent abroad from resident account. For best results, execute written Gift Deed for all high-value monetary transfers.

## 5. Can an NRI claim home loan tax benefits on Indian property?

NRIs can claim deduction up to ₹2 Lakhs on home loan interest under Section 24. Benefits apply if NRI has taxable income generated within India. Key operational metrics show legal status: fully permitted | authority: income tax act section 24 & 80c | condition: taxable income in india.

Principal repayment eligible under Section 80C up to ₹1.5 Lakhs. For best results, set off property loss against rental income in Indian IT return.

## 4. Can I invest in Public Provident Fund (PPF) as an NRI?

NRIs cannot open NEW PPF accounts after becoming non-residents. No extension permitted beyond 15-year maturity period for NRIs. Key operational metrics show legal status: restricted continuation | authority: ministry of finance | rule: no new ppf accounts.

Existing PPF accounts opened prior to becoming NRI can continue till 15-year maturity. For best results, maturity proceeds can be credited directly to NRO account.

## 3. Can an Indian resident open a foreign bank account while living in India?

Residents can open foreign bank accounts for holding LRS investments. Non-disclosure of foreign assets triggers severe Black Money Act penalties. Key operational metrics show legal status: permitted under lrs | authority: rbi fema guidelines | requirement: schedule fa disclosure.

Must disclose foreign bank details in Schedule FA of Income Tax Return. For best results, declare all foreign bank accounts annually regardless of balance.

## 2. Can an NRI invest in Indian Mutual Funds without visiting India?

NRIs can invest in Indian mutual funds online via paperless e-KYC. Select mutual fund houses (e.g. SBI, HDFC, ICICI) accept US/Canada NRI investments. Key operational metrics show legal status: permitted | authority: amfi / sebi | condition: kyc & us/canada rules.

US and Canada-based NRIs face restrictions due to FATCA compliance. For best results, complete FATCA self-declaration during online onboard process.

## 1. Can I withdraw entire EPF balance when moving abroad permanently?

Can withdraw full Provident Fund balance upon settling abroad permanently. Service under 5 years attracts income tax on employer contribution. Key operational metrics show legal status: permitted | authority: epfo rules | condition: 2 months unemployment / settlement abroad.

Tax-free if continuous service exceeded 5 years before leaving India. For best results, submit online EPF Form 19 via Universal Account Number (UAN) portal.
