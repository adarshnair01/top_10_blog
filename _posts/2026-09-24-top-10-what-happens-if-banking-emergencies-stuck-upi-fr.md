Digital payment adoption in India leads the world, but system glitches, unauthorized debits, and phishing attempts create panic. Knowing exact regulatory recourse and turnaround times guarantees financial recovery.

Evaluating banking emergencies requires referencing Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Ombudsman schemes, NPCI TAT circulars, and Cyber Crime reporting protocols.

## 10. What happens if your money is debited via UPI but receiver doesn't get it?

System failure triggers automatic reversal within T+1 working days. File grievance on NPCI portal if unresolved past 48 hours. Key operational metrics show rule: auto-reversal tat | authority: npci guidelines | penalty: ₹100/day for bank delay.

If bank fails to credit back within 5 days, bank pays ₹100 per day compensation. For best results, check UPI transaction status on BHIM app before re-sending money.

## 9. What happens if you fall victim to a digital cyber fraud or phishing call?

Report to 1930 Cyber Crime Helpline within 3 hours to freeze fraud account. Delaying beyond 3 days increases customer loss liability. Key operational metrics show rule: zero liability window | authority: rbi fraud circular & cyber helpline 1930 | golden window: 3 hours.

Notify bank within 3 days for ZERO customer liability. For best results, immediately block netbanking and debit cards via bank SMS numbers.

## 8. What happens if an ATM debits cash from your account but fails to dispense money?

ATM system logs transaction failure and flags auto-reversal. ₹100 per day penalty payable by bank for every day of delay beyond T+5. Key operational metrics show rule: t+5 days auto-credit | authority: rbi atm guidelines | penalty: ₹100/day after 5 days.

Bank must credit money back to customer account within 5 working days. For best results, save ATM slip and note exact ATM ID number.

## 7. What happens if you accidentally transfer money to the wrong bank account via IMPS/NEFT?

Inform your bank branch immediately with transfer UTR number. If recipient refuses consent, legal notice required for recovery. Key operational metrics show rule: inter-bank recovery | authority: rbi banking ombudsman | action: branch manager recall.

Remitting bank sends official recall request to recipient bank. For best results, double check account number and IFSC code before authorizing transfer.

## 6. What happens if your bank account is frozen due to Cyber Cell notice?

Bank places lien on account due to suspect transaction flagged in cyber police chain. Provide legitimate proof of funds to obtain NOC for lien removal. Key operational metrics show rule: lien clearance protocol | authority: state cyber cell & bank | action: investigating officer noc.

Must contact specific Cyber Police Station (IO) listed in bank notice. For best results, request bank for specific Cyber Cell FIR/Crime number and IO contact.

## 5. What happens if a loan EMI bounces due to insufficient funds?

Bank levies NACH/ECS bounce charge and lender adds late payment fee. Persistent default triggers recovery proceedings under SARFAESI Act. Key operational metrics show rule: nach bounce charges & cibil impact | authority: rbi / credit bureaus | charge: ₹250-500.

Delayed payment beyond 30 days negatively impacts CIBIL credit score. For best results, maintain adequate buffer balance 2 days prior to EMI auto-debit date.

## 4. What happens if your bank goes into RBI moratorium or liquidation?

DICGC insures bank deposits (savings, FD, RD) up to ₹5 Lakhs per bank. Amounts above ₹5 Lakhs settled upon bank asset liquidation. Key operational metrics show rule: ₹5 lakh insurance cover | authority: dicgc act | timeline: 90 days.

Depositors receive insured amount within 90 days of moratorium order. For best results, spread large cash reserves across multiple distinct banking licenses.

## 3. What happens if your credit card is used for unauthorized international transactions?

Block card immediately via mobile app or customer support. Turn off international online transactions on card app when not traveling. Key operational metrics show rule: immediate card block & dispute | authority: rbi security circular | safety: turn off int'l usage.

File credit card dispute form within 30 days for chargeback. For best results, disable international online usage by default in card settings app.

## 2. What happens if an online merchant double-charges your debit card?

Merchant system initiates refund for duplicate authorization hold. Bank issues temporary credit while investigating dispute. Key operational metrics show rule: merchant chargeback | authority: visa/mastercard/rupay rules | tat: 7-14 days.

If merchant fails, file chargeback dispute with issuing bank. For best results, provide invoice copy and bank statement showing double debit.

## 1. What happens if your fixed deposit (FD) matures and you don't renew it?

Unclaimed matured FDs earn savings account interest rate or original FD rate (whichever is lower). Funds remain safe in bank account until instructions received. Key operational metrics show rule: savings interest rate | authority: rbi fd guidelines | rate: lower of savings or original fd.

Auto-renewal facility prevents loss of higher term deposit yields. For best results, enable auto-renewal option while booking fixed deposits online.
