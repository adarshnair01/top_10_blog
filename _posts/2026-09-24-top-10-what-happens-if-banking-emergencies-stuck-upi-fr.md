Digital payment adoption in India leads the world, but system glitches, unauthorized debits, and phishing attempts create panic. Knowing exact regulatory recourse and turnaround times guarantees financial recovery.

Evaluating banking emergencies requires referencing Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Ombudsman schemes, NPCI TAT circulars, and Cyber Crime reporting protocols.

---

## 10. What happens if your money is debited via UPI but receiver doesn't get it?

*Key Specs: Rule: Auto-Reversal TAT | Authority: NPCI Guidelines | Penalty: ₹100/day for bank delay*

System failure triggers automatic reversal within T+1 working days. File grievance on NPCI portal if unresolved past 48 hours.

> **Operational Insight:** If bank fails to credit back within 5 days, bank pays ₹100 per day compensation. Check UPI transaction status on BHIM app before re-sending money.

## 9. What happens if you fall victim to a digital cyber fraud or phishing call?

*Key Specs: Rule: Zero Liability Window | Authority: RBI Fraud Circular & Cyber Helpline 1930 | Golden Window: 3 Hours*

Report to 1930 Cyber Crime Helpline within 3 hours to freeze fraud account. Delaying beyond 3 days increases customer loss liability.

> **Operational Insight:** Notify bank within 3 days for ZERO customer liability. Immediately block netbanking and debit cards via bank SMS numbers.

## 8. What happens if an ATM debits cash from your account but fails to dispense money?

*Key Specs: Rule: T+5 Days Auto-Credit | Authority: RBI ATM Guidelines | Penalty: ₹100/day after 5 days*

ATM system logs transaction failure and flags auto-reversal. ₹100 per day penalty payable by bank for every day of delay beyond T+5.

> **Operational Insight:** Bank must credit money back to customer account within 5 working days. Save ATM slip and note exact ATM ID number.

## 7. What happens if you accidentally transfer money to the wrong bank account via IMPS/NEFT?

*Key Specs: Rule: Inter-Bank Recovery | Authority: RBI Banking Ombudsman | Action: Branch Manager Recall*

Inform your bank branch immediately with transfer UTR number. If recipient refuses consent, legal notice required for recovery.

> **Operational Insight:** Remitting bank sends official recall request to recipient bank. Double check account number and IFSC code before authorizing transfer.

## 6. What happens if your bank account is frozen due to Cyber Cell notice?

*Key Specs: Rule: Lien Clearance Protocol | Authority: State Cyber Cell & Bank | Action: Investigating Officer NOC*

Bank places lien on account due to suspect transaction flagged in cyber police chain. Provide legitimate proof of funds to obtain NOC for lien removal.

> **Operational Insight:** Must contact specific Cyber Police Station (IO) listed in bank notice. Request bank for specific Cyber Cell FIR/Crime number and IO contact.

## 5. What happens if a loan EMI bounces due to insufficient funds?

*Key Specs: Rule: NACH Bounce Charges & CIBIL Impact | Authority: RBI / Credit Bureaus | Charge: ₹250-500*

Bank levies NACH/ECS bounce charge and lender adds late payment fee. Persistent default triggers recovery proceedings under SARFAESI Act.

> **Operational Insight:** Delayed payment beyond 30 days negatively impacts CIBIL credit score. Maintain adequate buffer balance 2 days prior to EMI auto-debit date.

## 4. What happens if your bank goes into RBI moratorium or liquidation?

*Key Specs: Rule: ₹5 Lakh Insurance Cover | Authority: DICGC Act | Timeline: 90 Days*

DICGC insures bank deposits (savings, FD, RD) up to ₹5 Lakhs per bank. Amounts above ₹5 Lakhs settled upon bank asset liquidation.

> **Operational Insight:** Depositors receive insured amount within 90 days of moratorium order. Spread large cash reserves across multiple distinct banking licenses.

## 3. What happens if your credit card is used for unauthorized international transactions?

*Key Specs: Rule: Immediate Card Block & Dispute | Authority: RBI Security Circular | Safety: Turn off Int'l Usage*

Block card immediately via mobile app or customer support. Turn off international online transactions on card app when not traveling.

> **Operational Insight:** File credit card dispute form within 30 days for chargeback. Disable international online usage by default in card settings app.

## 2. What happens if an online merchant double-charges your debit card?

*Key Specs: Rule: Merchant Chargeback | Authority: Visa/Mastercard/RuPay Rules | TAT: 7-14 Days*

Merchant system initiates refund for duplicate authorization hold. Bank issues temporary credit while investigating dispute.

> **Operational Insight:** If merchant fails, file chargeback dispute with issuing bank. Provide invoice copy and bank statement showing double debit.

## 1. What happens if your fixed deposit (FD) matures and you don't renew it?

*Key Specs: Rule: Savings Interest Rate | Authority: RBI FD Guidelines | Rate: Lower of Savings or Original FD*

Unclaimed matured FDs earn savings account interest rate or original FD rate (whichever is lower). Funds remain safe in bank account until instructions received.

> **Operational Insight:** Auto-renewal facility prevents loss of higher term deposit yields. Enable auto-renewal option while booking fixed deposits online.
